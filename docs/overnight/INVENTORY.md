### Layers

`controllers/` (routing, `ZodValidationPipe`, `ParseIdPipe`, one facade call) → `services/` (facade per module, plus helper services in `problems`) → `repositories/` (the only readers of `MockDatabase`) — with pure rules in `domain/` and row→API conversion in `mappers/`.

Cross-cutting (`apps/api/src/common/`):
- `guards/mock-auth.guard.ts`: global `APP_GUARD`. `Bearer jan|eva|petr`; **any other token logs in as jan**; no header gives 401.
- `filters/problem-details.filter.ts`: every error becomes `application/problem+json` (RFC 9457) with `code` and `requestId`. Registered in `main.ts`, not in `AppModule`.
- `pipes/zod-validation.pipe.ts`: first Zod issue gives 400 `VALIDATION_FAILED`.
- `pipes/parse-id.pipe.ts`: a bad Sqid in the path gives 404.
- `utils/ids.ts`: Sqids (`minLength: 5`, optional `SQIDS_ALPHABET`), canonical decode check.
- `utils/position.ts`: `positionAt(i)` gives `a0000`, `a0001`… The mock renumbers on every save.
- `database/mock-database.ts`: arrays `users`, `licenses`, `tags`, `chapters`, `problems`; `newProblemId()`. Seed in `mock-seed.ts` (3 users, 4 licences, 4 tags, 4 chapters, 7 problems).

`main.ts` adds the global prefix `api/v1` and the filter, and serves `/openapi.yaml` and `/docs` (Scalar). A test app has to repeat the prefix and filter setup, because `main.ts` calls `bootstrap()` at import.
